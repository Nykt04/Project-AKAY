# Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (BP 344) Comprehensive Accessibility Compliance Matrix
**The Law to Enhance Mobility of Disabled Persons (Philippines)**  
*Integrating Original BP 344 IRR (Appendix A), DPWH Guidelines, and NCDA Harmonized Standards*

---

## 1. Regulatory Context & Scope

Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (BP 344) mandates that buildings, institutions, establishments, and public utilities install architectural facilities and devices to enhance the mobility and barrier-free access of Persons with Disabilities (PWDs).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Ergonomic Baselines for Design:**
> - **Standard Manual Wheelchair Envelope:** 0.60 m – 0.75 m width × 1.10 m – 1.30 m length.
> - **Clear Wheelchair Turning Space:** Minimum 1.50 m diameter circle (1.80 m preferred in high-density public hubs).
> - **Comfortable Reach Range (Seated User):** 0.40 m (low reach) to 1.20 m (forward reach); side reach up to 1.30 m.
> - **Eye-Level Range:** 1.05 m to 1.25 m (seated); 1.40 m to 1.60 m (standing).

## 3. Project Implementation Cross-Reference

How the Civic Center and Commercial Shopping Mall prototype models (`src/civicPlan.ts` & `src/mallPlan.ts`) comply with BP 344:

1. **Ramp Geometry:**
   - External & Internal Ramps designed at exact $1:12$ slope ($8.33\%$), $1.20\text{ m}$ clear width, paired with intermediate landing every $6.0\text{ m}$.
   - Handrail dual-line geometry at $0.70\text{ m}$ and $0.90\text{ m}$ with $300\text{ mm}$ horizontal lead-ins.
2. **Doorways & Wall Offsets:**
   - Entrance and room portals sized between $1.20\text{ m}$ and $3.0\text{ m}$ clear width (exceeding BP 344 minimum of $0.80\text{ m}$).
   - Door junction nodes positioned directly on corridor side, avoiding double-back path routing.
3. **Dedicated PWD Restrooms:**
   - Sized at $2.50\text{ m} \times 2.50\text{ m}$ (exceeding code minimum $1.70\text{ m} \times 1.80\text{ m}$).
   - Outward-swinging $0.90\text{ m}$ door with internal clearance for full $1.50\text{ m}$ diameter turning circle.
   - Water closet centerline at $0.45\text{ m}$ from wall; L-shaped side grab bar and fold-up transfer bar at $0.75\text{ m}$.
4. **Service & Food Hall Counters:**
   - Concierge and Food Stall ordering counters set to $0.80\text{ m}$ height with $0.70\text{ m}$ knee recess.
5. **Auditorium / Cinema Wheelchair Bays:**
   - 4 designated $0.90\text{ m} \times 1.40\text{ m}$ wheelchair bays in Cinemas 1 & 2 adjacent to companion seating.
